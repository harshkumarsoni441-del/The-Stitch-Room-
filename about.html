Our Story

A Legacy of Craftsmanship

The Stitch Room began with a simple idea: to bring high-quality, perfectly fitting garments to schools and institutions across Tamil Nadu. Over the years, we have grown from a small tailoring unit into a state-of-the-art textile manufacturing hub in Vellore.

Our journey is defined by our commitment to quality, precision, and customer satisfaction. By bridging traditional tailoring techniques with modern manufacturing technology, we ensure every piece of clothing we produce meets the highest standards of durability and comfort.

Mission & Vision

Driving Excellence in Apparel

Our Mission: To provide top-tier school uniforms and custom clothing across Tamil Nadu through premium fabrics and perfect fits, making quality accessible to all.

Our Vision: To become the most trusted and innovative institutional clothing partner in India, known for sustainable practices, technological integration, and unparalleled craftsmanship.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding school partnerships, ordering processes, corporate requirements, and uniform customizations.

Topics

School Partnerships

How can our school partner with The Stitch Room?

Schools can submit a partnership enquiry via our Contact page. Our team will schedule a consultation to discuss your uniform requirements, design needs, and onboarding process.

What are the benefits of a school partnership?

Partner schools receive a dedicated 3D catalogue, automated parent ordering portals, bulk discounts, and custom branding options.

Uniform & Customization

Can we customize the fabric and colors?

Yes, we offer extensive customization options including fabric choice (cotton, poly-viscose, etc.), exact pantone color matching, and custom embroidery for logos.

What sizes are available?

We manufacture all standard sizes for Junior and Senior categories. Custom tailoring and Made-to-Order sizes are also available upon request.

Parent & Customer Guidance

How do I order uniforms for my child?

Parents can log into the Customer Portal, select their child's school, view the approved catalogue, and place an order directly online.

What is the return/exchange policy?

Standard sizes can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Custom-fitted items are non-refundable unless there is a manufacturing defect.

Ordering & Bulk Information

Is there a minimum quantity for bulk orders?

For initial bulk manufacturing, we typically require a minimum of 100 pieces per design. However, reorders through the parent portal have no minimum limits.

What are the payment options for bulk orders?

Bulk orders require a 50% advance payment via Bank Transfer, NEFT, or corporate cheque. The balance is due upon dispatch.

Corporate & Hospital Requirements

Do you supply hospital scrubs and lab coats?

Yes, we manufacture medical-grade scrubs, lab coats, and patient gowns with fluid-resistant and anti-microbial fabric options.

Can we get custom uniforms for our corporate staff?

Absolutely. We design and supply premium executive wear, daily staff uniforms, and security attire for organizations of all sizes.

School-Network Onboarding

How long does it take to onboard a new school?

From design approval to setting up your 3D showroom and parent portal, the onboarding process takes approximately 2-3 weeks.

Do you provide sample uniforms before finalizing?

Yes, once the digital 3D designs are approved, we create physical samples for the management's final approval before starting bulk production.
